The problem

Rural students lose school days when a bus is cancelled, full or never comes. Private transport is costly, and families have little information about what is running.

Our solution

We predict demand, show seat availability, and when a bus fails, match students from nearby villages to verified shared vehicles, with admin approval and parent visibility.

How it works

  1. A student registers with a village, a college and parental consent if under 18.
  2. The planner shows buses, seats and the predicted shortage.
  3. If a bus is cancelled or full, the student requests an alternative ride.
  4. An admin approves the vehicle; the student and parent are notified.
